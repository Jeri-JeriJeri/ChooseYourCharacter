
**Spider-Man (Peter Parker)**

* Defeated the **Sinister Six**, repeatedly taking on six supervillains at once.
* Held up a **collapsing building/structure** through sheer strength, demonstrating his superhuman physical power.
* Defeated **Venom**, despite Venom possessing Spider-Man’s powers plus additional abilities and immunity to Spider-Sense.
* Fought and defeated extremely powerful Marvel characters while relying on **intelligence, Spider-Sense, agility, and improvisation** rather than raw power.
* Joined the **Avengers** and fought alongside some of Marvel's most powerful heroes.
* Survived and fought through battles involving **Galactus-level and cosmic threats** in various comic storylines.
* Created his own advanced technology, including his **web-shooters and specialized web formulas**, while balancing superhero work with science and engineering.

**Dorian Yates**

* Won **6 consecutive Mr. Olympia titles (1992–1997)**, making him one of the most successful bodybuilders in history. ([Guinness World Records][2])
* Won the **1992 Mr. Olympia** in his second appearance at the competition after finishing 2nd in 1991. ([Wikipedia][3])
* Went undefeated at the **Mr. Olympia from 1992 through 1997**, winning every Olympia he entered during that six-year reign. ([Fitness Volt][4])
* Became known for an exceptionally **thick and developed back**, helping define the massive "mass monster" era of bodybuilding. ([Wikipedia][5])
* Weighed **116.57 kg (257 lb) at the 1993 Olympia**, earning the Guinness World Records mark for heaviest Mr. Olympia champion at the time. ([Guinness World Records][2])
* Won the **1991 Night of Champions**, establishing himself as a major professional bodybuilding contender before his Olympia run. ([Fitness Volt][4])
* Won his **sixth Olympia in 1997 despite tearing his left triceps three weeks before the competition**, then retired from competitive bodybuilding. ([Wikipedia][6])

**Dean Potter**

* Became the **first person to solo the Supercanaleta route on Cerro Fitz Roy** in Patagonia in 2002. ([Wikipedia][7])
* Completed the **first free solo ascent of Heaven** on Glacier Point in Yosemite. ([Wikipedia][7])
* Completed the **first FreeBASE ascent of the Eiger's Deep Blue Sea**, combining free climbing with a BASE parachute. ([Wikipedia][7])
* Set a **2:36:45 speed record on The Nose of El Capitan** in 2010, one of the world's most famous big-wall climbing routes. ([Climbing][8])
* Was part of the **first Yosemite Triple Crown**, linking Half Dome, Mount Watkins, and El Capitan in a single day. ([Climbing][8])
* Completed a four-hour speed solo of **Half Dome's Regular Northwest Face**, climbing approximately 95% of it free solo. ([Climbing][8])
* Free-soloed numerous extremely difficult Yosemite routes, including **Astroman, Separate Reality, Blind Faith, and Heaven**. ([Climbing][8])

**Revenant — Apex Legends**

* Was originally the **Mercenary Syndicate's greatest hitman** before being turned into a simulacrum. ([EA][9])
* Was repeatedly **resurrected as a simulacrum**, effectively denying him the normal limits of human mortality.
* **Hunted down the people responsible for turning him into Revenant**, eventually killing the individuals involved in his transformation. ([EA][9])
* Became capable of surviving extreme damage thanks to his **simulacrum body**, while retaining the skills of an elite assassin.
* His programming eventually failed, allowing him to **discover the truth about his existence** and turn against the people who controlled him. ([EA][9])
* Possesses enhanced **wall-climbing, movement and assassination abilities**, including Shadow Pounce and Assassin's Instinct. ([EA Help][10])
* Can create **Forged Shadows that absorb damage and regenerate**, with his abilities refreshing when he knocks enemies down. ([EA Help][10])

**Future Gohan**

* Became the **strongest surviving Z Fighter in his devastated future**, after most of Earth's defenders had been killed by the Androids.
* Reached **Super Saiyan**, despite having to grow up in a future where Goku and most of the other Z Fighters were already dead.
* Fought the **Androids 17 and 18** repeatedly despite being dramatically outnumbered and outmatched.
* Continued protecting **Future Trunks**, training him and passing on the hope of defeating the Androids.
* Eventually made the ultimate sacrifice by fighting the Androids **alone to give Trunks time to survive and continue training**.
* His death became the major catalyst for **Future Trunks' transformation into Super Saiyan and determination to defeat the Androids**.
* His actions helped create the chain of events that eventually allowed Trunks to return to the past and **change the future**.
