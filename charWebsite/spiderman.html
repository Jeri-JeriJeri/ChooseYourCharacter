Spider-Man

Peter Benjamin Parker is a young man who gained superhuman abilities after being bitten by a spider and fights crime in New York City as the superhero Spider-Man.

In his teens, Parker attended Midtown School of Science and Technology, where he quickly became best friends with his classmate Ned Leeds, and the target of bullying from Flash Thompson. Parker also became acquainted with Michelle Jones, who often teased him, and developed a crush on Liz Allan, a popular student. In late 2015, at the age of fourteen, Parker was bitten by a spider and acquired superhuman powers, including strength and speed proportional to that of a spider and an uncanny ability to adhere to walls. He also acquired a precognitive sense known as Spider-Sense, although it worked intermittently.

Accomplishments

  • Defeated the Sinister Six, repeatedly taking on six supervillains at once.
  • Held up a collapsing building/structure through sheer strength, demonstrating his superhuman physical power.
  • Defeated Venom, despite Venom possessing Spider-Man’s powers plus additional abilities and immunity to Spider-Sense.
  • Fought and defeated extremely powerful Marvel characters while relying on intelligence, Spider-Sense, agility, and improvisation rather than raw power.
  • Joined the Avengers and fought alongside some of Marvel's most powerful heroes.
  • Survived and fought through battles involving Galactus-level and cosmic threats in various comic storylines.
  • Created his own advanced technology, including his web-shooters and specialized web formulas, while balancing superhero work with science and engineering.
