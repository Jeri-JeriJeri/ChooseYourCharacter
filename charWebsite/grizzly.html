Kyriakos Grizzly Kapakoulak

Weightlifter and strongman from the northern Greek city of Kavala who has found a following on YouTube for his impressive strength. His training has been considered unconventional since it combines strongman, powerlifting, and weightlifting techniques. .

He has amassed over 360,000 subscribers on his YouTube account where he shares his unusually large weightlifting gains. His first YouTube video from December 2012 featured him performing a seated pin press of 180 kilograms. He has been trained by boxer Giannis "Hercules" Stoforidis. His work in the world of strength has been commended by bodybuilder Ronnie Coleman.

Accomplishments

  • Performed 4 Zercher shrugs with 1,080 lb (490 kg) loaded on the bar — his signature and probably his most ridiculous strength feat.
  • Became famous for pioneering/popularizing the Zercher shrug, an extremely unusual movement that became closely associated with him.
  • Was a former Olympic weightlifter, developing his strength base before becoming known internationally as “Grizzly.”
  • His strength became an internet phenomenon, with his Zercher-shrug videos spreading widely and major fitness personalities such as Jujimufu and Ronnie Coleman reacting to or featuring his training.
