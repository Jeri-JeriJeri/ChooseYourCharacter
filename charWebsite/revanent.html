Revanent

Revenant is an Assault Legend. He was introduced in Season 4 and is locked from the base game. He can be unlocked using digital currency: either Legend Tokens 12,000 or Apex Coins 750, or by buying the Champion Edition downloadable content. Revenant received a complete rework in Season 18.

Revenant used to be human. He used to be the greatest hitman the Mercenary Syndicate ever had. He used to look in the mirror and see his human face looking back. But time changes everything, and when his programming finally failed, he saw what he had become at the hands of the Mercenary Syndicate and Hammond Robotics: a walking nightmare of steel and vestigial flesh. His masters resurrected him as a simulacrum, snatching him from death’s embrace again and again and programming him to forget. He hunted down every last person who did this to him, but the last thing he wanted, he couldn't have: the sweet release of death. Unable to die without destroying his preserved human head, he was on the hunt to find and deal with it.

Accomplishments

  • Was originally the **Mercenary Syndicate's greatest hitman** before being turned into a simulacrum.
  • Was repeatedly **resurrected as a simulacrum**, effectively denying him the normal limits of human mortality.
  • **Hunted down the people responsible for turning him into Revenant**, eventually killing the individuals involved in his transformation.
  • His programming eventually failed, allowing him to **discover the truth about his existence** and turn against the people who controlled him.
